## Changes

Glyph isolation and boundary trimming; centreline/source-outline extraction; noise removal; quadratic outline conversion; size and pen-weight normalization; spacing/metrics/OpenType kerning; alternate A; composed accents and smart aliases. Lowercase j has a disclosed normalized editorial tittle. Short note joins are contemporary inferred bridges, not authenticated historical ligatures.

## Inferred forms and exclusions

58/62 alphanumerics are source-traced; X/Z/6/7 are inferred. Period, comma, !, ?, apostrophe and dash are traced. The 32 unverified basic forms and seven accent marks use contemporary image-assisted designs. The v0.8 master supplies X/6/7/£/Z/&/+/=/double quote. These are designs in the style of the reviewed hand, not evidence that Doyle wrote those specific forms. Exact v0.8 prompt, generated master and style reference are included; vector provenance identifies the earlier inferred designs.

Secretary-written letters, printed notebook tables, blue cataloguing marks and uncertain folio digits are excluded. Previous unresolved-source font versions, downloaded scans, reference-paper figures and personal research notes are excluded from this public snapshot. No code from the research paper's AGPL synthesis engine is incorporated.
